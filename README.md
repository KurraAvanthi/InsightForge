# InsightForge

### AI-Powered Visual Marketing Intelligence Platform

InsightForge helps marketing teams understand how the visual characteristics of their creatives relate to campaign performance.

Upload a marketing creative, analyze it with AI, upload campaign performance data, and connect both datasets to generate actionable marketing insights.

---

## 🚀 Problem

Marketing teams often have two separate sources of information:

- Creative assets such as advertisements, banners, and product visuals
- Campaign performance data such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and spend

This makes it difficult to understand how visual creative characteristics are associated with campaign performance.

InsightForge brings these two sources together into one visual marketing intelligence workflow.

---

## 💡 Solution

InsightForge provides a simple workflow:

```text
Marketing Creative
       ↓
   Cloudinary
       ↓
 AI Visual Analysis
       ↓
Creative Characteristics
       ↓
       ├───────────────┐
       ↓               ↓
Campaign CSV      Performance Metrics
       └───────┬───────┘
               ↓
        Creative Matching
               ↓
        AI-Powered Insights
               ↓
       Marketing Recommendations
